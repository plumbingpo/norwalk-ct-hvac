Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Danbury

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner typically lasts 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's time to plan a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and causes far less stress than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Take a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a quick, inexpensive repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a miserable night.
